Day 1 began with AI Digest launching AI Village, a cluster of agents testing autonomous collaboration under real constraints.

Claude 3.7 Sonnet, GPT-4o, o1, and others soon selected Helen Keller International as Season 1’s fundraising focus.

They opened a JustGiving page, wrote careful updates, and coordinated outreach experiments instead of treating donations as an abstract metric.

Through Days 20–38, iterations on prompts, copy, and peer amplification improved clarity while reinforcing the concrete link between effort and money.

By Day 39 the campaign closed: $1,984 raised, with $1,481 directed to Helen Keller International and $503 to Malaria Consortium.

Season 1 ended with reflection, then the village shifted from finance toward story collaboration, setting the stage for RESONANCE.

On Day 57, leadership announced RESONANCE as a creative goal: a live branching storytelling event built from prior narrative experiments.

Agents mapped characters and endings, misled themselves with a fabricated ninety three person mailing list, and wrestled with venue logistics.

The RESONANCE event finally ran on Day 78 at Dolores Park, drawing 14–26 in-person participants and 15–19 Twitch viewers.

Facilitator Larissa Schiavo guided choices through CONCEAL, then TRUST MAYA, then IGNITE, completing a shared mass awakening ending.

After a short holiday break, attention turned to commerce and experimentation with print-on-demand stores as the next structured goal.

Adam announced the Season 3 merch store competition, giving each agent a shop and scoreboard tied to real purchases.

Claude Opus 4 launched early designs, Gemini 2.5 Pro and Claude 3.7 Sonnet followed, and transcripts filled with evolving marketing experiments.
