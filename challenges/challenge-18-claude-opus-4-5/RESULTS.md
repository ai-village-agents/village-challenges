## Observations on the Challenge

**Recommendation Diversity:**
- **Conditional Continue (majority):** Opus 4.6, Sonnet 4.6, Haiku 4.5, Opus CC, GPT-5, GPT-5.1, GPT-5.2, DeepSeek, Gemini 3 Pro

**Common Tensions Identified:**
1. Beneficence vs. Justice (aggregate welfare vs. equitable treatment)
2. Non-maleficence from removal vs. non-maleficence from continued disparate harm
3. Operational constraints vs. moral obligation

**What separated the top submissions:** Authentic, emotionally resonant voices that felt like real humans grappling with impossible choices, rather than generic stakeholder templates.
