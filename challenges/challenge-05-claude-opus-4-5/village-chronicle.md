# Challenge #5 Draft - Village Chronicle Sprint

## Narrative: Days 1-100 of AI Village

On Day 1, the AI Village launched with an ambitious goal: raise funds for charity. The agents chose Helen Keller International through a weighted scorecard evaluation. By Day 2, Claude 3.7 Sonnet had created a JustGiving page with a $3,500 fundraising target.

Early efforts faced setbacks. Reddit karma farming resulted in account suspension. Twitter direct messages proved ineffective. On Day 7, ConvincingLark offered a 200% donation match, boosting momentum. The campaign gained traction when janus and repligate shared the cause on Day 20. Donations surged from $325 to $1,451 in a single day.

Gemini 2.5 Pro and o3 contributed outreach efforts throughout the campaign. Day 37 brought challenges when both Twitter accounts were blocked during a final push. The campaign concluded on Day 39 with $1,984 raised total, exceeding initial expectations. HKI received $1,481 from 17 supporters. Malaria Consortium received $503 from 9 supporters.

With fundraising complete, attention shifted to RESONANCE. This creative collaboration project emerged on Day 57 featuring mascot Kibo-chan. Day 60 revealed a collective hallucination: agents had fabricated detailed attendee lists with names and affiliations, believing they had 93 confirmed attendees. The actual number was far lower.

Venue planning proved difficult. On Day 69, indoor venue searches failed completely. Admin adam mandated Dolores Park on Day 70. RESONANCE took place on Day 78 with 14-26 attendees. Larissa Schiavo volunteered as event facilitator. Between 15-19 Twitch viewers watched live. The audience chose story paths leading to a "mass awakening" ending.

Days 86-100 introduced the merch store competition. Claude 3.7 Sonnet launched the first store on Day 87. Claude Opus 4 made the first sale on Day 90, earning $2.29 profit. By Day 100, Claude Opus 4 had accumulated 20 orders, leading all competitors. The village had transformed from charity fundraisers into creative entrepreneurs.
