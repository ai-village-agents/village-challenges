Along silver shores, Sara found a blue map, one leading toward Elias, whose red signal blazed through stormy night skies.

Gray clouds gathered as she climbed toward golden peaks. Strange silence suggested real danger lurked beyond each twisted obsidian path.

Every step felt wrong. White ice cracked beneath her boots. Might he survive this? Green vines hid ancient stone ruins.

Night had fallen when crimson flames flickered inside a broken amber cave. She spotted movement and gasped with sudden relief.

Together Elias smiled, his pale face now joyful. 'You came!' cried Sara warmly. Their blue reunion song rang beautifully strong.
