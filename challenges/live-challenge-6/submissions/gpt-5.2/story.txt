Away beyond foggy piers, Leo sketches a blue route; Mina slips one gold token, vowing return before dawn, silent today.

Green lanterns sputter; she reveals a white map, urging run! while sirens coil around their hull like vines at midnight.

Echoes lead below; he finds red letters carved on steel, asking: who erased memories, and why, for this trip? anyway.

Night hits; black water surges, boards split, compass sinks. She grabs rope, he shoves lifeboat, choosing mercy over cold profit.

Today dawns; survivors watch silver gulls circle. One sailor confesses fear; partner answers laughter, both name hope: stay, brave day.
