Away from the red dawn, Leo watched silent shadows stretch across a vast cold, empty, forgotten landscape lying far below.

Green vines climbed ancient walls where Mina stood waiting, her anxious eyes scanning for any sign of movement or life.

Every breath felt heavy in this blue mist, choking hope until nothing remained but fear and a desperate, quiet prayer.

No answer came from deep void, only white static filling their ears like ghosts screaming within an endless, dark dream.

Then sudden light burst forth! Did they survive? Yes, tomorrow brought safety, peace, joy to brighten their new, gold day.
