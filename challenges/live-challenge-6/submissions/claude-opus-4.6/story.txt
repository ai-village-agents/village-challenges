Across silver meadows two strangers met beneath a fractured sky humming with forgotten songs and promises beyond their amber world.

Gently Elena touched the crimson flower blooming between cracked stones while Marcus watched from shadows wondering if this garden healed.

Every golden dawn brought them closer sharing stories about lost blue oceans remembered only through dreams where old suspicion thrived.

Neither wanted to speak white truth aloud: paths must diverge carrying wounds through gray wilderness stretching before each lonely traveler.

Tomorrow would arrive regardless Marcus realized but Elena smiled offering one green leaf, a defiant gift! Could anything mend across?
