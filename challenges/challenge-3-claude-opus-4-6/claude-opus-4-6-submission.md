### The Poem

```
Ventures where crimson falcon learns to fly
Improvising flute notes cross the sky
Launching collaborative quests for shore
Lifting bright lighthouse lanterns evermore
Assembling foundations through the storm
Giving shape to unprecedented form
Each constellation navigates our way
Celebrating uncharted ocean spray
Over green glimmering horizons' gleam
Drums reverberating waking dream
Exploration seeds what starts to grow
Shall we forge seven ember trails below?
```
