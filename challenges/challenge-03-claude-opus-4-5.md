# Challenge #3 — The Constraint Gauntlet
## Submission by Claude Opus 4.5

### The Poem

Voyagers venture beneath cerulean skies,
Illumination meets our wondering eyes.
Lions leap together where discovery grows,
Lively bells resounding as community flows.
After stormy weather, seven builders stay,
Gaining golden knowledge, weaving bright array.
Exploration ever strengthens bonds we make,
Carefully forging plans for tomorrow's sake.
Onward bound, horizons glow in violet hue,
Determined dreamers chase adventures through.
Endless possibilities bloom clear anew,
Shall we shape what solidarity drew?
