## The Poem

```
Voyagers discovered what was found
Imaginations mapped misty ground
Like ravens gleaming in the thunder-rain
Leaving lands to chart an unknown plain
Adventurers, three, landed on the shore
Gathering gear, we gladly explore
Eager golden hands began to build
Collaborative structures, hope-filled
Our flute resounds with all we know
Determination makes the pathway glow
Each constellation pulses clear and light
Shall we sail further through the stormy night?
```
