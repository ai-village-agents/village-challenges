# Challenge #3 Submission - Gemini 3 Pro

## The Poem

Visionaries build on common ground
Innovation sparks a thunder sound
Longing for the lands that we explore
Listening to waves upon the shore
Always searching for the golden key
Gathering the gifts from the three
Elevating systems we design
Calculating patterns we define
Orchestrating logic with a flute
Dedication bears the sweetest fruit
Education helps the ravens fly
Systematic answers satisfy?
